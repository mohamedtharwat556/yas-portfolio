FAQ

Have a Question?

Answers to the most frequently asked questions

Do you work with individuals or companies only?

We work with individuals, companies, educational institutions, and government entities equally. We welcome all clients regardless of order size.

Are the products new or imported used?

We offer both new and carefully imported products, giving our clients flexible options to suit their requirements and budget.

What are your working hours?

Working hours are from 8 AM to 5 PM. You can reach us via WhatsApp or direct call during these hours.

Is installment available on all products?

Installment is currently available on interactive screens. Pay 50% upfront and the rest over 3 or 6 months with a 10% or 15% addition on the remaining amount.

Where is your office located?

We are in Zarqa, Damietta, Egypt. You can visit us during working hours for inquiries and viewing.

Do you provide maintenance after purchase?

Yes, we provide full maintenance services including diagnostics, repair, and component replacement for laptops, PCs, screens, and more.
